⚙ ProMail Tracker — Settings

Must start with https:// (spam filters distrust http pixels). No trailing slash needed.

How it stays out of spam

  1. Only one 1×1 image is added to each email — nothing else in your message is changed.
  2. The pixel URL is clean HTTPS with no query strings, redirects, or cookies.
  3. No hidden display:none tricks — just a plain tiny image, the most filter-friendly form.
  4. If the server is down, your email is sent completely untouched.

Note: the pixel itself is spam-neutral. Inbox placement still depends on your sending domain's reputation (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), subject lines, and sending volume — keep those healthy as usual.
